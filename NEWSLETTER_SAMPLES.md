# Newsletter samples

Collected 2026-11-15. The segment is an analytics newspaper about work:
one finding, one chart, a reader who has to act. These are the desks
to measure issue 27 against. A new sample replaces a weaker one. It
does not become a second list.

The source of a number sits in the line under the chart: who was read,
how many openings, the date. It is not its own section. Shivam,
2026-11-15.

| Desk | Piece | What to take |
|---|---|---|
| [Indeed Hiring Lab](https://hiringlab.indeed.com/2026/10/22/us-labor-market-snapshot-october-2026/) | US Labor Market Snapshot, October 2026 | Opens on one picture ("low-hire, low-fire"), then a short block per indicator. Each block says where the number is and which way it is moving. |
| [Financial Times, The AI Shift](https://www.ft.com/content/3d2669e3-c05e-48c9-8bb3-893c1d66de2e) | "Where are all the job losses?", Burn-Murdoch and O'Connor, 23 October 2025 | One question, answered in the first screen. The limit of the data is a sentence inside the answer. |
| [Mint Plain Facts](https://www.livemint.com/topic/plain-facts) | Weekday data section. The contract-hiring piece (353 BSE 500 companies, FY26) puts 17% in the first lines and names the comparison, 11% three years earlier. | The number a person will repeat is in the opening. The chart is the argument. |
| [The Hindu Data](https://www.thehindu.com/data/) | Datapoint. The chart is the story; the column is a caption for it. | A graphic has to stand alone on a phone. That is the carousel. |
| [LinkedIn Economic Graph](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/) | Hiring Rate, indexed, with the definition in the line under the chart. India is one of the three series. | One line of method, under the figure, then back to the story. |

We do not copy their datasets. Indeed tracks a postings index and wages.
LinkedIn tracks hires on member profiles. Mint reads annual reports and
official surveys. Myro reads live openings on company career pages.
The form is what we take. The count stays ours.
