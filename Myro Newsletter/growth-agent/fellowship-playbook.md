# Fellowship Playbook

**Status:** launch-gated until company registration is ready.

The fellowship system is designed now so it can launch quickly later, but public internship language should not be used until Myro is ready to support it as a company program.

## Positioning

**Myro Campus Growth Fellowship**  
For students and recent graduates who want real growth experience while helping peers understand the AI-era job market.

## Structure

- Up to 5 founding fellows.
- Each fellow owns a region, college cluster, or community cluster.
- Fellows manage relationships with campus captains, class reps, club partners, alumni groups, and placement-cell contacts.
- Captains share approved links and help host lightweight signup/onboarding drives.

## Audience

- College students.
- Recent graduates.
- Early-career job seekers who need a skill and role direction.

## Rewards

- Founding Fellow internship title.
- Public leaderboard.
- Certificate.
- LinkedIn recommendation.
- Myro channel shoutouts.

## Simple Leaderboard

| Action | Points |
|---|---:|
| Verified Myro account created | 1 |
| Onboarding or CV upload completed | 3 |
| Myro result, score page, profile, or referral link shared | 5 |

The leaderboard should show total points, accounts, activations, and shares. Keep the public view simple; use internal checks for suspicious activity.

## Fellow Weekly Rhythm

### Monday

- Pick college/community targets.
- Set personal account and activation goals.
- Share approved weekly campaign links.

### Wednesday

- Review which groups or captains are working.
- Replace weak messaging.
- Ask for help where a campus needs a stronger intro.

### Friday

- Update leaderboard.
- Highlight one useful fellow learning.
- Prepare weekend signup/onboarding push.

### Sunday

- Collect feedback from fellows and captains.
- Decide next week's challenge theme.

## Outreach Policy

Be persistent, useful, and direct. Do not be deceptive, repetitive, or irrelevant.

Allowed:

- Posting in relevant student and career groups.
- DMing classmates, peers, alumni, and warm contacts.
- Asking group admins and club leads for permission.
- One thoughtful follow-up.
- Referral link sharing.
- Campus captain resharing.
- Event invitations and signup/onboarding drives.

Not allowed:

- Fake accounts.
- Misleading claims.
- Mass scraped DMs.
- Repeated messages after no response.
- Posting where promotion is banned.
- Pressuring users to upload CVs.
- Buying email lists.
- Claiming official college endorsement without confirmation.

## Fraud And Quality Checks

Flag patterns such as:

- many signups from one device or network with no activations,
- repeated throwaway-looking emails from one fellow,
- accounts created but never opened again,
- share-loop points with no reachable shared link,
- captains promising rewards or outcomes Myro has not approved.

Do not publicly shame a fellow or captain. Investigate privately, correct incentives, and remove invalid points if needed.
