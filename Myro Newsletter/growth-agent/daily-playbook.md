# Daily Playbook

This playbook runs `content_growth_mode` until the fellowship launch gate opens.

## Daily Rhythm

### Morning: Pick The Claim

Choose one sharp, useful career claim from:

- an existing newsletter issue,
- Myro jobs data,
- a chart in the newsletter dashboards,
- a real community question,
- or a pattern from yesterday's results.

The claim must be concrete enough to defend. Good claims use numbers, role names, skills, sectors, or geographies.

Examples:

- "Data Engineer is a bigger AI-era target than the AI Engineer title."
- "India's AI jobs are not only in Big Tech."
- "Your resume may be aimed at the loudest market, not the hiring market."

### Midday: Create Distribution

Prepare 2-4 pieces:

- one LinkedIn post,
- one Instagram/Facebook post,
- one Substack or newsletter note when useful,
- one short X post or thread when the channel is active.

Each draft goes in `outbox/` and includes the required fields from `outbox/README.md`.

### Afternoon: Seed Communities

Find 3-5 relevant discussions where the answer can be genuinely useful.

Priority communities:

- Hacker News career/hiring threads,
- Reddit career and India tech communities,
- LinkedIn comments under job-market posts,
- college WhatsApp or Telegram groups,
- Discord communities for students, builders, and job seekers.

Community replies should lead with help, not a CTA. Mention Myro only when it adds context.

### Evening: Write The Growth Brief

Create `reports/daily-YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

Include:

- accounts created yesterday, last 7 days, and total,
- onboarding or CV completions yesterday, last 7 days, and total,
- best converting source,
- weakest source or CTA,
- top 3 drafted or published assets,
- what needs Shivam's review,
- tomorrow's experiment.

## Required Post Preview Format

Every scheduled or review-ready post must include:

```text
Channel:
Publish time:
Review deadline:
Status:
CTA URL:
UTM source:
UTM medium:
UTM campaign:
UTM content:
Source claim:
Copy:
```

## CTA Rules

- Use `/signup` as the default path.
- Include `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`.
- Match `utm_campaign` to the content-calendar campaign slug.
- Keep one dominant CTA per post.
- Never claim guaranteed jobs, guaranteed results, or official college endorsement.

## Daily Quality Bar

Before a draft becomes `review-ready`, check:

- The first line has a real hook.
- The claim is traceable.
- The CTA is clear.
- The post sounds like Myro, not generic AI career advice.
- The link can be measured.
- The outreach policy is respected.
