HiMyro Mobile Experience Feedback

 *First Impression* 
The website looks modern and professional. The dark theme with teal highlights gives it an AI/tech-product feel, which immediately creates trust. The value proposition "A job-ready CV in 10 minutes" is clear and easy to understand.
 *What Works Well* 
✅ Clear CTA The CV upload box is visible immediately without scrolling. As a new user, I instantly know what action I need to take.
✅ Simple Messaging "10 minutes • Any Job • Scored • Tailored" quickly communicates the benefits without overwhelming the user.
✅ Modern Branding The logo, typography, and overall UI feel premium compared to many resume-builder websites that look outdated.
✅ Privacy Mention The word "Private" below the upload section helps reduce hesitation when uploading personal documents.
 *What Felt Confusing* 
⚠️ Myro vs Resume Builder At first glance, I wasn't sure whether:
Myro creates resumes from scratch,
improves existing resumes,
stores multiple versions,
or acts as an AI career coach.
The core differentiation isn't immediately obvious.
⚠️ No Visual Workflow As a first-time visitor, I'd like to see:
Upload CV → Get Score → Create Versions → Apply
A simple visual journey would make the product easier to understand.
⚠️ Skill Detection Banner The "Skill detected: Stakeholder Mgmt L4" banner is interesting but confusing.
Questions that came to mind:
What does L4 mean?
Is that good or bad?
How is it calculated?
Why should I care?
Adding a tooltip or short explanation would help.
 *Mobile UX Improvements* 
📱 The upload box could be slightly larger.
📱 A "See Sample Result" button would increase trust before uploading a resume.
📱 Student users may want examples like:
Marketing Resume
Software Resume
Finance Resume
Fresher Resume
before committing to sign up.
📱 A short demo video on the homepage would help users understand the product within 30 seconds.
 *What Would Make Me Use It Regularly* 
I would use HiMyro if it helped me:
Manage all resume versions in one place
Tell me which CV version fits a specific job
Track applications linked to each CV version
Show skill gaps for target roles
Suggest improvements before applying
Without these features, I might use it once and leave.
 *Go-To-Market Insight* 
The biggest pain point for students is not creating a resume.
It's managing multiple resumes.
Most students have files like:
Resume_Final.pdf
Resume_Final2.pdf
Resume_Updated.pdf
Resume_Internship.pdf
Resume_Marketing.pdf
HiMyro should market itself as:
"Your Resume Operating System."
or
"One Master CV. Unlimited Job-Specific Versions."
This positioning is much more memorable than simply saying "AI Resume Builder" because there are already hundreds of AI resume builders in the market.

 *Overall Rating* 
Design: 8.5/10
Ease of Understanding: 7/10
Trust Factor: 8/10
Mobile Experience: 8/10
Likelihood to Use: 8/10 (if version management and job-matching features are strong)

- *To conclude with* HiMyro has a polished and professional first impression, but it needs to communicate its unique value proposition—resume version management and job-specific optimization—more clearly within the first few seconds of a user's visit.
