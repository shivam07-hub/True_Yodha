We pulled more than 35,000 active India roles from company career pages between 27 April and 4 June 2026, and one name near the top of the employer list is neither a bank nor an IT-services giant. Siemens posted **1,461 active roles** — more than Wipro (998) and more than Accenture (962). Cognizant leads the slice at 1,705, so Siemens is not number one. But an industrial engineering group out-hiring two-thirds of the Indian outsourcing trio is the signal most "where is hiring" coverage misses.

### TL;DR

- Siemens has **1,461 active roles**, second only to Cognizant (1,705) and ahead of Wipro and Accenture.
- It is spread across cities: **Bengaluru 410, Pune 213, Gurugram 187, Thane 157**, plus Goa, Mumbai and Aurangabad.
- The role mix is genuinely mixed: **Manufacturing 302, IT & Infrastructure 295, Software Engineering 247, Sales & Marketing 176**.
- Roughly a fifth of the batch is manufacturing and another fifth enterprise IT — this is not a pure-tech employer.

Most feeds run the line that India hiring is led by IT services and banks. Broadly true — but Siemens slots in at number two, which means an engineering employer is competing for the same candidates as the outsourcing majors, across a wider range of skills.

The practical read: stop treating "Siemens" as one job. Manufacturing is its single largest domain, but IT, infrastructure and software combined are larger still. A controls engineer, a backend developer, an enterprise-IT specialist and a supply-chain analyst are all looking at the same employer — and Siemens is more spread across cities than a typical software firm, so a non-metro posting genuinely widens your options. The bridge that pays is industrial software: anything connecting a physical process to a digital system, from PLC and SCADA to MES, digital-twin and IoT platforms. Pick the lane your CV actually fits before you apply.

Myro's jobs dataset, active roles read from company career pages, scrape window 27 April to 4 June 2026: 34,959 active roles across 247 employers. The Siemens figures cover 1,461 roles tagged to Siemens at query time, normalised to Myro's role-domain taxonomy. A point-in-time snapshot, not a complete count.
