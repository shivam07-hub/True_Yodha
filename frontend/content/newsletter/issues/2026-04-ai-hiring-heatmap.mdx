We pulled **9,446 active listings across 60 enterprise employers** from April 11–19, 2026. The story isn't the one the timeline is telling.

### TL;DR

- **397 AI/ML/Data roles** across the dataset — about 4% of all open roles tracked.
- **Banks are the #1 AI buyer.** BFSI + Fintech = **124 active AI roles**. Pure tech doesn't break the top 5.
- **"Prompt Engineer" postings: 0.** Across 9,446 listings. Zero.
- **The hiring is in India.** Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune dominate every US metro in our data.
- **Analytics + Data Engineer = 56% of all AI roles.** Companies aren't hiring "AI" — they're hiring people who can move and reshape data.

The one move to make this week: pick the role from the heatmap that's one step adjacent to what you do today, then compare your CV against the skills that market is actually requiring — not just listing.
