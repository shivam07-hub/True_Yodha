The NCR gold search now has a clean next step: expand from 13 loaded companies to a 20-company watchlist. The first 13 produced 245 jobs and 124 strong-fit roles. The next seven add payments, consulting, research, healthtech and Gurugram-heavy advisory signals.

For readers, the useful move is a short watchlist, refreshed weekly and tailored by lane.

### TL;DR

- The loaded set starts with **13 companies** and **245 verified jobs**.
- The next seven companies to add are **American Express, Mastercard, Bain & Company, PwC India, Gartner, Paytm and Innovaccer**.
- The best first five remain **ZS Associates, Adobe, Microsoft, Google and McKinsey & Company**.
- Use the 20-company list as a weekly tracking system.

## How to use the watchlist

Start with five companies. Pick one lane per company: analytics consulting at ZS, product analytics at Adobe, Data & AI at Microsoft, partnerships or cloud at Google, transformation analytics at McKinsey.

Refresh the watchlist every week. Closed roles disappear quickly, but the skills repeat. If three companies ask for business analytics, stakeholder management and cloud/data fluency, your CV should show that proof before the next application batch.

## What this means for you

If you are early-career, use the list to find analyst roles with clear proof requirements. ZS, Genpact, Adobe, Cognizant and Innovaccer are good first checks.

If you are mid-career, build two CV versions: one for consulting/strategy and one for data/product. Apply where the job description matches one of those two versions.

If you are senior, prioritise roles where client ownership, data fluency and transformation delivery sit together. Microsoft, McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte, Bain, Gartner and American Express are the first places to watch.

## The one move to make this week

Create a five-company watchlist from the table above, compare your CV against those roles, and fix the top three skill gaps before applying.
