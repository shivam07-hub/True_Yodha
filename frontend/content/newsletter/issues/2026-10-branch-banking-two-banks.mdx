If you want a branch job in a big metro, apply to Axis Bank. If you want that job in Surat, Indore or Rajkot, apply to Kotak.

We read 220+ MNC career pages and pulled close to 23,000 openings, on 9 September 2026. 3,138 of them are frontline branch-banking roles. Axis Bank holds 65.1% of that lane, 2,042 postings. Kotak Mahindra Bank holds 10.4%. Sixty-plus other employers hold 24.5% between them. Technology, across four role domains in the same read, is 6,270 openings. This lane is one domain.

Axis posts 0.0% of its roles outside the big metros. Kotak posts 51.9% of its own roles there.

| Place | Openings | Axis | Kotak | Both |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kolkata | 194 | 89.2% | 3.1% | 92.3% |
| Chennai | 287 | 84.3% | 4.2% | 88.5% |
| Ahmedabad | 176 | 83.0% | 5.1% | 88.1% |
| Delhi NCR | 616 | 81.2% | 3.6% | 84.7% |
| Pune | 170 | 77.6% | 3.5% | 81.2% |
| Hyderabad | 249 | 69.1% | 3.2% | 72.3% |
| Mumbai | 563 | 62.2% | 7.6% | 69.8% |
| Bengaluru | 519 | 63.0% | 6.2% | 69.2% |

360 more sit outside those metros, 11.5% of the lane.

## If you are applying in Kolkata, Chennai or Ahmedabad, the posting is Axis

The two banks together hold 92.3% of Kolkata's branch openings, 88.5% of Chennai's and 88.1% of Ahmedabad's. Delhi NCR is the largest city in the lane, 616 openings, and the two banks hold 84.7% of them. Mumbai is 69.8% and Bengaluru 69.2%. Axis is the majority in both: 62.2% of Mumbai, 63.0% of Bengaluru.

The cities outside, the ones with a name and a count, are smaller and they are real. Surat 17, Indore 12, Rajkot 9, Jaipur 7, Kochi 7, Lucknow 6, Vadodara 6, Jalandhar 5. That list is Kotak's map.

## If you are starting out, Kolkata hires at 1.7 years. Outside the metros the ask is 3.6

Kolkata's branch jobs are 71.4% entry level, at 1.7 years on average. Humidity included. Delhi NCR is 51.5% entry at 2.7 years, and that city is 19.6% of the whole lane. Mumbai is 42.6% entry, also at 2.7 years, and 17.9% of the lane. Chennai is 47.8% entry at 2.0 years.

Bengaluru asks 4.3 years and is 25.8% entry. Pune is 34.2% entry at 2.8 years. Hyderabad is 28.6% at 3.2 years. Ahmedabad is 43.2% at 2.2 years. Outside the metros the share of entry-level roles is 6.9%, on those 360 postings, at 3.6 years. The early door is a metro door, and Kolkata opens it widest.

## If you write one CV for this lane, write it for Business Development Associate

Business Development Associate (RB-LS) is 763 openings, all of them at Axis, 24.3% of the lane. It is 100% entry level, at 1.0 year. One title, a quarter of every frontline branch posting we read.

The other names, in the employer's own words: Branch Relationship Officer, 346, Axis. Assistant Manager Sales – Corporate Salary, 189, Axis. Teller, 119, Axis. Inbound Sales Representative – Voice, 50, Accenture. Relationship Manager – Priority Banking, 33, Axis. Elite Banker – Branch Banking, 24, and that one is Kotak.

## Sales, banking products, and a mutual fund

These postings name sales (1,721), banking (1,258), lead generation (1,206), financial services (1,160), business development (1,012), banking products (789), relationship building (683), mutual funds (582), general insurance (561) and retail banking (520).

Put those on the CV. Send it to Axis if the city is a metro, and to Kotak if the city is Surat or Indore.
