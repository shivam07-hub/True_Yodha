We tracked **30,000+ jobs from MNC career pages** and analysed a 9-day April window of 9,446 active listings across 60 employers. Five things the data says that popular career coverage doesn't.

### TL;DR

- **Pune outranks Hyderabad.** 768 Pune jobs vs. 396 Hyderabad in our analysed slice. Five companies, led by CMA CGM and Alstom, are quietly building global hubs there.
- **Pharma is hiring at Big Tech scale.** Sanofi + Novartis = 1,000 roles in a single batch. Almost no career coverage about it.
- **Soft skills are window dressing.** "Cross-Functional Collaboration" appears 229 times. Primary requirement: 3 times. "Quality Culture": 179 times. Primary: 0.
- **Accenture alone posted 1,000 jobs on one day.** The outsourcing trio (Accenture, Cognizant, DXC) = 1,594 jobs — more than all Big Tech names in the dataset combined.
- **85% of analysed jobs came from one Saturday batch.** If you're refreshing portals mid-week, you're mostly looking at noise. Set a Saturday alarm.

The one move: pick a role and a city from this data, then run your CV against what those companies actually mark as *required* — not just listed.
