**If you want risk work in India, apply to the Big Four.** EY, Deloitte and KPMG post half the openings, and they ask for regulation, audit and a CA.

**If you came for risk analytics, go to the banks with Python and statistics.** That seat is real and it is small: 45 titles.

Our agents read 260+ MNC career pages and checked close to 46,500 live openings in India on 20 October 2026. 1,868 of them were risk and compliance roles. Below is what those postings ask for, split by who posts them.

| Skill | All | Big Four | BFSI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regulation | 67.2% | 60.3% | 82.6% |
| Audit | 50.6% | 47.2% | 50.4% |
| Risk management | 43.8% | 36.5% | 51.5% |

## If you want the Big Four, start with EY

EY posts 575 risk and compliance openings, 30.8% of the lane on its own. Deloitte posts 242 (13.0%) and KPMG 126 (6.7%). Together the three hold 943 openings, 50.5% of everything we read.

More than half of EY's openings, 305, sit in one practice, Business Consulting Risk. Forensics (56), Assurance (51) and Tax (43) follow. Deloitte's risk lane is security work: 79.3% of its postings name cyber or information security.

The Big Four ask for a CA more than anyone else in this lane: 25.9% of their postings, and 34.8% of EY's. If you cleared CA and want to work on regulation, this is the door that opens widest.

These employers named regulation (569), audit (445), risk management (344), cyber security (254), a Chartered Accountant qualification (244) and forensics (144).

## If you want a bank, apply to Axis in Mumbai

Axis Bank posts 231 risk openings, 37.2% of everything banks and financial firms posted in this lane. Deutsche Bank follows with 61, JP Morgan Chase 54, CRISIL 36, State Street 33, Kotak Mahindra Bank 29, Barclays 27 and American Express 25.

Axis is hiring for transaction monitoring and credit desks. Its largest titles are FCI:DTM Analyst (31), in its Digital Transaction Monitoring department, Credit Officer in SBB Credit (18), RCU Regional Onboarding (14) and Compliance Transaction Monitoring (13), in the bank's own wording.

Bank risk jobs sit in Mumbai: 52.8% of them, and 73.2% of Axis's. The regulator is a local train away.

Banks named regulation (513), risk management (320), audit (313), Excel (186), an MBA (174), fraud (130), AML and KYC (122) and credit risk (116). Regulation appears in 82.6% of bank postings and 92.2% of Axis's.

## If you came for risk analytics, 45 titles carry the word

45 of the 1,868 titles say analytics, 2.4% of the lane. Axis posts 11 of them, EY 7, Deloitte 6, Wells Fargo 5, Morgan Stanley 4 and JP Morgan Chase 3. Two of the 45 are entry level.

The usual advice for risk analytics is SQL, Python, SAS and Basel. We checked it against the lane. Python appears in 11.0% of all risk postings, and SAS and Basel in 2.8% each. Inside the 45 analytics titles, the picture changes: Python is in 51.1% and SAS in 33.3%.

Those 45 postings named statistics (24), Python (23), credit risk (19), SQL (15) and SAS (15).

## If you are starting out, a bank is the shorter door

180 openings are entry level, 9.6% of the lane, at an average minimum of 0.7 years. Axis posts 39 of them, EY 35, Genpact 21, Deutsche Bank 11, JP Morgan Chase 8 and American Express 7. At banks and financial firms, 13.8% of risk roles are entry level. At the Big Four, 5.1% are.

The entry-level postings named regulation (142), risk management (73), audit (64), fraud (54), an MBA (47) and AML and KYC (45).

## The rest of the map

Consulting as a whole posts 58.3% of the lane, and banks and financial firms 33.2%. Beyond the Big Four, Genpact (51), Infosys (28) and Wipro (26) post the most. Mumbai holds 34.7% of the lane, Bengaluru 25.8% and Delhi NCR 19.6%. The Big Four spread across all three, at 32.3%, 26.0% and 25.5%.
