245 roles came out of a private Delhi NCR target-company search on 27 May 2026. The public signal is useful for anyone applying around consulting, analytics, product, cloud, data and GTM roles: 13 companies loaded cleanly, 245/245 roles were enriched with skills and role domains, and 124 roles cleared the strong-fit screen.

We are not publishing the user name, CV, email, profile, account id or private job intent. The map below is the community version of the search.

### TL;DR

- The NCR gold set contains **245 unique jobs** across **13 loaded companies**.
- **124 roles** were marked strong-fit after enrichment and ranking.
- The strongest first-pass companies are **ZS Associates, Adobe, Microsoft, Google and McKinsey & Company**.
- The cleanest lanes are consulting, analytics, product, cloud/data and GTM work.

The first five are the cleanest starting set because they combine brand fit, role relevance and location practicality. BCG has the highest strong-fit count, but many of its best roles are Bengaluru or Mumbai-heavy. Genpact has the largest loaded volume and a practical Noida signal, which makes it valuable for applicants who want roles close to NCR alongside premium logos.

## What the data says about the search

This job set rewards a focused CV. Broad "strategy plus analytics plus AI" language needs proof behind every claim. The repeated patterns are consulting, business transformation, business analytics, product analytics, AI business solutions, customer strategy and cloud/data advisory.

For NCR candidates, Noida and Gurugram matter in different ways. Noida shows up through Adobe, Genpact, EY India, HCL and Publicis Sapient. Gurugram appears through BCG and Google signals, and it should expand further once American Express, Gartner, Bain, Mastercard and Paytm are added to the watchlist.

## What this means for you

If you are early-career, pick one company and one role family. ZS analytics consulting, Adobe product/business analysis, Genpact technology advisory and Cognizant data/product roles are cleaner entry lanes than a broad 13-company blast.

If you are mid-career, work from proof. A CV with SQL, Python, Tableau, Snowflake, customer strategy, GTM or transformation evidence should lead with the business problem solved, then show the tool list.

If you are senior, the strongest angle is ownership across data, client work and change. Microsoft, McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte and Adobe all reward candidates who can show stakeholder management plus delivery.

## The one move to make this week

Choose five companies from this set and tailor one CV version for each lane: analytics consulting, product analytics, cloud/data consulting, GTM strategy or technology advisory. Start with the companies where your proof already exists.

## Methodology

This issue uses the NCR gold search completed on 27 May 2026. The set verified **245 unique jobs** in Supabase, **245/245** with `main_skills`, **245/245** with `role_domain`, and **2,536** `job_skills` rows. The ranked lead file contains 75 top-fit roles. Deloitte route labels were cleaned to `Deloitte India`; Publicis multi-city duplicates were merged. Local LM Studio enrichment used `google/gemma-3-4b`. No user-identifying fields are included in this public issue.
