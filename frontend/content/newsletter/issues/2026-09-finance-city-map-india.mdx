22% of India's finance openings sit in Mumbai, and 32 companies hold all of them.

Our agents read 220+ MNC career pages this month and pulled close to 23,000 openings. 2,067 of them are finance, risk and compliance roles in India. Career pages belonging to one firm are counted once, so EY's and Deloitte's several pages consolidate to the parent brand.

## Mumbai holds the finance floor, and a short list of employers holds Mumbai

Mumbai has 21.9% of India's finance openings, which is 453 roles. They come from 32 employers, 14.2 roles each, the deepest per-employer market in the country. Three employers hold 51.7% of the city: Axis Bank with 113, EY with 74 and Deloitte with 47. JP Morgan Chase adds 34 and Morgan Stanley 29. Entry level runs 19.3%, the highest of any big metro, and the average posting asks for 4.9 years. The roles are business analysis (45), risk management (39), banking services (35) and investment management (25). What those employers name is financial services (91), risk management (77), regulatory compliance (71), internal controls (71), chartered accountant (47) and wealth management (46). A short list of employers means a short list of applications, each one worth writing properly.

## Bengaluru has more finance openings and twice as many doors

Bengaluru holds 24.8%, which is 512 roles, more than Mumbai. They come from 65 employers at 7.9 each. The largest are EY (101), Deloitte (78), Goldman Sachs (36), JP Morgan Chase (35) and Wipro (26), and the top three hold 42.0% of the city against Mumbai's 51.7%.

The postings read differently too. Bengaluru's most-named skill is SQL (81), ahead of financial services (80), regulatory requirements (69), regulatory compliance (69), Python (61) and financial statements (56). Its largest role families are business operations (44), business analysis (34) and cybersecurity (31). A finance job in Bengaluru is a finance job run as analytics.

## Delhi NCR is one firm's market

NCR holds 17.9%, which is 371 roles from 32 employers. EY posts 147 of them, 39.6% of every finance opening in the metro, and the top three employers hold 58.0%. It is the most mid-level market in the country at 43.2%, with senior and above taking 38.7%, the lowest anywhere.

NCR names regulatory compliance (76), liquidity management (55), chartered accountant (48), SQL (49) and financial services (47).

## Pune and Hyderabad run the back office

Pune holds 6.6% and is 63.0% senior and above, the most experienced finance market in India. BNY Mellon posts the most roles there (21), ahead of EY (21) and Citibank (16). The skills are accounting (19), balance sheet (15) and reconciliation (14).

Hyderabad holds 11.1% from 28 employers, led by Deloitte (52), Genpact (37) and EY (36). Entry level is 8.0%, the lowest of the big metros, and the roles are regulation and legal compliance (20), process improvement (20) and accounts payable and receivable (15).

Both cities do the work that keeps a balance sheet correct, and both ask you to arrive knowing how.
