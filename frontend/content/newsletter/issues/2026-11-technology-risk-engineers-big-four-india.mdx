**If you are a software engineer looking for a way into risk, the seat is technology risk, and the Big Four post most of it.** Deloitte, EY and KPMG post 65.4% of these roles, and 58.2% of them ask for an engineering degree.

**If you want to keep writing code, risk teams hire engineers too.** Goldman Sachs posts 7 of the 23 build roles in the lane.

| Skill | Tech risk | Rest of lane |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering degree | 58.2% | 15.1% |
| Cloud | 49.3% | 15.5% |
| Identity and access | 39.4% | 11.3% |
| CISA, CISSP and similar | 47.6% | 7.9% |
| ISO 27001, NIST and similar | 46.2% | 4.6% |
| Audit | 62.3% | 54.4% |
| Risk assessment | 50.3% | 25.7% |
| Data privacy | 31.8% | 6.9% |
| Python | 8.6% | 16.6% |
| Credit risk | 0.3% | 12.7% |

Source: Myro, every live risk and compliance role on 260+ MNC career pages in India, read 16 November 2026. 292 technology-risk roles against the other 821, each role counted once however many cities it is posted in.

## If you are an engineer, the Big Four is where the seat is

Deloitte posts 131 technology-risk roles, 44.9% of all of them. KPMG posts 31 (10.6%) and EY 29 (9.9%). Infosys (11) and Cognizant (9) come next, and the banks post a handful each.

Deloitte's largest practice here is Cyber Strategy & Transformation, with 55 roles. Its audit and controls teams have 29, Detect & Respond 22 and Digital Trust & Privacy 12. Across all 292, the titles split into GRC and ISO 27001 work (46), IT audit and controls (43), digital and technology risk (34), third-party risk (29), threat and incident response (22), data privacy (15) and identity and access (13). A title can fall in two.

At the Big Four, 72.3% of these roles ask for an engineering degree.

These roles named audit (182), risk assessment (147), cloud (144), CISA, CISSP and similar (139), ISO 27001 and similar frameworks (135), and identity and access (115).

## If you write code, the cloud and access lines already count

Cloud appears in 49.3% of technology-risk roles, against 15.5% across the rest of the lane. Identity and access appears in 39.4%, against 11.3%. Those lines already sit on many engineers' CVs.

Python appears in 8.6% and SQL in 7.9%, about half their share in the rest of the lane. These roles ask about the systems you ran: the cloud, who gets access to it, and the controls around both.

## Add one framework and one certificate

ISO 27001 is named in 102 roles and NIST in 87. Across the rest of the risk lane, frameworks of this kind appear in 4.6% of roles. CISSP appears in 102, CISA in 95 and CISM in 58.

The usual advice for an engineer moving into risk runs through credit-risk models. We checked it against these roles: credit risk appears in 1 of the 292.

These roles named CISSP (102), ISO 27001 (102), CISA (95), NIST (87) and CISM (58).

## If you have three to five years, you are this seat's level

200 of the 292 roles state a minimum experience. 130 of those (65.0%) ask for five years or less, and 74 (37.0%) for three or less.

13 are entry level. DSCI and SANS reported in May that entry-level cyber roles are thinning, and in these postings 4.5% are.

## If you want to keep writing code

23 build roles sit inside the risk lane: engineers who build the controls, data pipelines and access tools that risk teams run on. Goldman Sachs posts 7, Wipro 3, American Express 2 and Deloitte 2.

These roles named cloud (13), Python (11), Java (9) and SQL (8).

## The rest of the map

Bengaluru has 44.9% of technology-risk roles, which saves most engineers a move, if not the commute. Mumbai has 23.6% and Delhi NCR 18.2%, then Pune (11.3%) and Hyderabad (10.3%); a role posted in two cities counts in both. 39.4% of these roles were first posted in the 30 days before we read them.
