75 ranked leads from the NCR gold set give a sharper job-search signal than the full 245-role table. **Strategy & Consulting accounts for 37 leads. Data & Analytics adds 21 more.** Together, those two lanes make up 58 of the 75 strongest ranked roles.

That is the useful filter for applicants with a business, analytics or transformation profile. Start with the lanes where the market is already returning matches.

### TL;DR

- The ranked file contains **75 top job leads** from the 245-role NCR set.
- **37 leads** sit in Strategy & Consulting and **21** sit in Data & Analytics.
- BCG, Deloitte India, Genpact, Microsoft, Monitor Deloitte, Adobe, Google and Cognizant carry most of the ranked list.
- The repeated skill signals are consulting, business transformation, business analytics, product analytics, advanced analytics and AI innovation.

## The role-domain split

| Role domain | Ranked leads | What to prove |
|---|---:|---|
| **Strategy & Consulting** | **37** | Client work, transformation, market sizing, GTM, stakeholder leadership |
| **Data & Analytics** | **21** | SQL/Python, analytics stories, dashboards, forecasting, business impact |

| Product Management | 4 | Product analytics, customer insight, roadmaps, execution |

The strongest pattern is a consulting and analytics map with enough technical adjacency to reward candidates who can connect data, client work and implementation.

The CV move is straightforward: convert tool claims into business examples. "Built Tableau dashboard" is weaker than "built a sales dashboard used by a regional lead to reprioritise accounts." "Used Python" is weaker than a project where Python changed a forecast, a workflow, or a client decision.

## What this means for you

If you are early-career, target analyst lanes first: ZS analytics consulting, Adobe business analyst, Genpact technology advisory, BCG data analyst or Cognizant data/product roles. Bring one clean project and one business explanation.

If you are mid-career, the best bridge is consulting plus analytics. Put your strongest client, operations, growth, data or transformation story in the top half of the CV.

If you are senior, focus on ownership roles: AI business solutions, cloud/data architecture, client experience, account growth, responsible AI or transformation leadership.

## The one move to make this week

Pick two role families from this issue: Strategy & Consulting and Data & Analytics. Build one CV version for each, then apply only where the job title and required skills match that version.
