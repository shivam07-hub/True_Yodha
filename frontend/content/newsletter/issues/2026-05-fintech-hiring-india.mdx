**257 roles** across Razorpay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED and Intuit. Fintech is not one job market — it's five very different hiring surfaces.

### TL;DR

- **Paytm leads with 111 roles** — product, growth, sales, finance and lending.
- **Razorpay (50) + Intuit (47) + PhonePe (43)** follow. CRED is small but signal-dense: data, risk, money movement.
- **Fintech is broader than engineering.** Software Engineering = 42 roles. Product Management = 52. Sales & Marketing = 52. Finance = 42.
- **Best candidate focus:** payments, product, platform, data, risk and financial operations — one company, one function.

Pick one company from this list and one function. Then compare your CV against what that lane actually requires before you apply.
