**1,016 semiconductor roles** across AMD, Intel, NXP, Qualcomm, STMicroelectronics and Texas Instruments — May 20 scrape. The cleanest signal: Qualcomm + AMD account for 609 of those roles, and Bengaluru + Hyderabad account for 870.

### TL;DR

- **Qualcomm leads (350)**, followed by AMD (259), NXP (168), TI (136), Intel (99).
- **Bengaluru (538) + Hyderabad (332) = 85.6%** of the batch.
- **Software Engineering (404 roles)** beats Manufacturing (62). This is a software-heavy semiconductor batch.
- **Top required skills:** Design Verification (131), Real-Time Computing (89), Analog Design (66), Physical Design (44), Embedded C (39), Linux Kernel (25).
- **Two clear entry lanes:** design verification/firmware for hardware backgrounds; Linux/drivers/real-time for software backgrounds.

Pick one lane — verification, embedded, analog, firmware, or platform software. Then compare your CV against the exact skills this batch requires.
