Two Indian banks are running a quarter of the job market. Kotak and Axis have 3,490 openings between them, out of the 14,851 we read across 217 employers. Thirteen of those 3,490 are engineering jobs.

The roles are Branch Relationship Officer, Business Development Associate, Branch Head, Teller, Personal Banker. Axis alone has 234 open Branch Relationship Officer posts and 83 Tellers. It's a real hiring wave. It is almost entirely people who will stand in a branch.

Accenture and Kotak have almost the same number of openings: 1,832 and 1,892. Accenture's include 1,261 engineering roles. Kotak's include 12. Two employers the same size on paper, running completely different companies underneath, and every job count on the internet treats them as equal.

| Employer | Openings | Engineering titles |
|---|---|---|
| Kotak Mahindra Bank | 1,892 | 12 |
| Accenture | 1,832 | 1,261 |
| Axis Bank | 1,598 | 1 |
| Everyone else (214 employers) | 9,529 | 2,692 |

This is why job counts lie to you. When someone says India has thousands of openings this month, a quarter of that number is two banks staffing branches. If you're an engineer, your number is 4,362, not 14,851, and nobody separates the two for you.

You can see it before you click. Axis hires through ripplehire.com, Kotak through oraclecloud.com. Those two systems hold 4,331 openings and 312 engineering roles. Workday and the chip-maker career pages hold about the same pile, 4,518, and 2,468 of those are engineering. Same corporate apply form. Wildly different odds.

| System | Openings | Engineering | Who's behind it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phenom / Eightfold | 859 | 70.5% | Micron, NVIDIA, Qualcomm |
| Workday | 3,659 | 50.9% | Accenture, Genpact |
| SAP | 1,870 | 11.9% | EY, Deloitte |
| Oracle | 2,658 | 10.5% | Kotak, JP Morgan |
| RippleHire | 1,673 | 2.0% | Axis Bank |

The software isn't doing this. Kotak bought Oracle and Axis bought RippleHire years ago, and the job mix behind each link is the employer showing through. Four systems carry 66.4% of everything on the list, and each one is dominated by one or two very large employers.

## What this means for you

**Early-career (0-3 years).** Branch banking is genuinely open right now and it hires without experience. Axis has 245 Business Development Associate posts and 234 Branch Relationship Officer posts live. If you have been firing CVs at engineering roles for six months with nothing back, this is a door that is actually open, and it is worth knowing that before you spend another six.

**Mid-career (3-10 years).** Both banks are hiring managers, not only front-line staff: 195 Branch Head and 156 Branch Operations Head posts at Axis. If you already run a team in retail, telecom or insurance, that is a lateral move into a sector hiring at volume this month.

**Senior (10+ years).** Read the concentration, not the total. In a market where two employers hold 23.5% of the openings, your next role probably comes through someone who already works at one of them. Pick the two or three names that matter and work the people, not the portals.

## The one move to make this week

Count what is open in your role, not what is open. Then decide whether four months of silence has been about your CV or about the size of the pile you were applying into.

## Methodology

14,851 job openings read from 217 companies' own career pages on 8 August 2026, across 46 distinct application systems. Counts come from a single scrape date rather than a live-status filter, so a posting counted here may have closed since.

Role buckets come from regular-expression matching on the job title as the employer wrote it. "System" is our scraper's adapter name, which is close to but not exactly a vendor identity. The Phenom adapter also covers Eightfold-hosted career sites, so read that row as a group of chip-maker and large-enterprise pages rather than one product.

Two caveats. This is 217 large employers with their own career sites, so startups, agencies and anything hiring only through job boards are absent. And a single date is a snapshot: Kotak and Axis are running a hiring wave right now, and this issue does not tell you how long it lasts.
