If you're applying to Citibank or Wells Fargo, the listing in front of you is almost certainly real. If you're applying to AB InBev or Palo Alto Networks, give the Ghost Index thirty seconds before you tailor a CV to it.

We checked ten global employers hiring in India against their own hiring systems: a role counts closed once the system stops serving it, and we check whether the careers-page ad comes down with it.

| Employer | Closed roles watched | Still advertised | Avg days still up after close |
|---|---|---|---|
| Citibank | 188 | 3.4% | 2.6 |
| Wells Fargo | 85 | 5.1% | 10.9 |
| Amazon | 61 | 10.6% | 0.0 |
| Barclays | 370 | 13.2% | 11.5 |
| Nike | 35 | 17.1% | 11.3 |
| Capgemini | 58 | 23.6% | 37.9 |
| Cisco | 271 | 29.4% | 5.5 |
| Okta | 38 | 39.5% | 42.4 |
| Palo Alto Networks | 44 | 50.0% | 7.6 |
| AB InBev | 23 | 69.6% | 11.5 |

Four employers sit under 15%. Four sit over 29%, and two of those average more than five weeks before a dead ad comes down. This is the same list of global employers, hiring through the same kind of applicant tracking systems, with genuinely different housekeeping habits.

## If you're applying to Citibank, Wells Fargo, Amazon or Barclays, trust the listing

These four pull a dead ad fast — Citibank in 2.6 days on average, Amazon the same day it closes. A posting on their careers page today is a role that's hiring today.

Citibank, Wells Fargo, Barclays and Amazon are asking for SQL (57 mentions across the four), regulatory compliance (55), Java (49), Python (40) and supply chain (12). A bank's technical floor and a retailer's technical floor turn out to be the same floor: the same query language, the same two programming languages, showing up in a compliance job and a logistics job alike.

## If you're applying to Cisco, Okta, Palo Alto Networks or AB InBev, check before you tailor a CV

AB InBev leaves seven in ten closed roles up. Okta's dead ads average 42 days before anyone pulls them. Closing a hiring system and pulling the job-board ad are two different teams' jobs at most companies, and one of them is slower.

These four are asking for Python (90 mentions across the four), the Okta platform itself (37, worth knowing if you're applying there specifically), artificial intelligence (30), computer science fundamentals (27) and software engineering (23).

## Nike and Capgemini sit in the middle

Nike pulls a dead ad in about 11 days. Capgemini takes closer to 38. Either is worth a Ghost Index check once a listing is a few weeks old, cheaper than an evening spent tailoring a CV for a role that's already filled.

Nike's India roles ask for software engineering, computer science fundamentals and SQL. Capgemini's skew toward SAP S/4HANA, digital transformation and data migration: an implementation-consulting list, distinct from the product-engineering skills above.
