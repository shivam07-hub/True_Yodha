Let me save you some worry this fortnight, because that is the whole reason we watch this market for you.

When Myro Agent went through the career pages in the second week of July 2026, one thing stood out. 843 active India postings named "Agentic AI" as a required skill. A quarter ago that number was one or two. So if your first instinct is that you are already behind on some new skill everyone suddenly wants, sit down first. We looked properly, so you do not have to.

Here is what we found, and it is the reason we never hand you a headline number raw. 499 of those 843 postings came from a single company that had pasted the phrase into almost everything. Take that one company out, and 344 postings remain. Those 344 are the ones worth your attention, because they are spread across 73 different employers in 8 industries. Not the loud number. The honest one.

### TL;DR

- 843 postings named "Agentic AI" and 923 named "Artificial Intelligence" in a single two-day analysis. Both barely registered in spring.
- One company wrote 499 of the 843. Remove it and **344 remain, across 73 firms and 8 industries** — that is the real signal.
- The demand is cross-industry: consulting, product technology, BFSI and industrial firms all name it.
- "Agentic AI" on a posting is an applied engineering skill, not model training. For most engineers the gap is a handful of skills.

## The number, honestly

| Skill tag | Postings (12–13 July) |

Read raw, "Agentic AI" shows up on roughly 1 in 35 of the ~29,600 postings Myro Agent analysed in that run. Once the single-company padding is removed, the genuine, spread-out demand is closer to 1 in 86. Still remarkable for a skill that was not on postings a quarter ago.

Both readings tell you something. The inflated number tells you the phrase has entered recruiter vocabulary. The clean number tells you where the actual work is. We show you both, because you deserve to see the market the way we see it, not the way a headline dresses it up.

## Where the real demand sits

Take out the one company and the skill is not concentrated. It is scattered, which is exactly what a genuinely new skill looks like before it settles into a few teams.

| Sector | Firms naming Agentic AI |

| Consulting | EY, Cognizant, Deloitte, Infosys, Mphasis |
| Product technology | Salesforce, Boomi, Glean, HighRadius |

No single sector owns it. That is the tell worth trusting. When a skill turns up across consulting and banks and factories in the same quarter, it is not a passing fashion. It is a new baseline forming, and you have time to get on the right side of it.

## What "Agentic AI" on a posting actually asks for

The phrase sounds like it needs a research background. It does not, and this is where a lot of good engineers talk themselves out of a door that is open to them. Decoded, a posting asking for Agentic AI wants some mix of the following.

How an agent loop actually runs — tool calls, stop conditions, retries. Evaluation-harness design, which is scoring agent runs the way you already score tests. Guardrails, meaning budget caps, output validation, and circuit breakers. And cost and latency budgeting, so a non-deterministic system stays affordable.

If you already write software, that is an extension of what you do, not a change of career. The scarce part is reliability instinct and systems thinking, and if you have that, you are further along than you think.

## What this means for you

If you are early in your career, this is a rare open door. A named, in-demand skill that almost nobody can honestly claim five years of. Do not chase a certificate. Build one real agent end to end, instrument it, and learn what breaks. Depth is what will set you apart here, and depth is available to a beginner in a way it rarely is.

If you are mid-career, do not wait for your title to change before you start. Learn one framework properly, then bring an agent into whatever your company is already shipping. That is how you quietly become the person who ships agents inside your current job, before the market forces the move on you.

If you are further along, the gap in most organisations is not building agents. It is operating them reliably, and that maps straight onto skills you have spent a decade sharpening. Own that function. It is where teams currently lose the most time and money, which means it is where your experience is worth the most.

Right now: run your current role and "Agentic AI" through Myro to see your real, skill-by-skill gap. Not the buzzword. The checklist you can actually work through.

## Methodology

Counts are active India postings in the Myro jobs dataset naming each skill as a tag, from Myro Agent's work analysis of 12–13 July 2026 — roughly 29,600 postings across 200 employers. Skill tags are extracted per posting and imperfect, and the single-company concentration in "Agentic AI" is exactly why we split it out rather than report the headline alone. Myro carries no salary data. "Agentic AI" is an industry term for AI systems that act in loops; it is not yet a standardised job title.
