We tracked 34,959 active India roles from company career pages between 27 April and 4 June 2026. **Bengaluru holds 8,991 of them — 25.7%, close to one in four.** The next city, Hyderabad, has 2,665. Bengaluru is not leading the map; it is the map. No other Indian city is within three times its volume.

For anyone deciding where to base a job hunt, this is the most useful number in the dataset. It does not mean you must move — it means that outside Bengaluru you are working a structurally smaller market, and should plan accordingly.

### TL;DR

- Bengaluru holds **8,991 of 34,959 roles — 25.7%**, the largest single-city share by far.
- Hyderabad (2,665), Pune (2,221) and Mumbai (2,029) are the next tier — each under a third of Bengaluru's volume.
- The employer mix is broad: **Infosys 515, Wipro 439, Siemens 410, HP/HPE 286, Alstom 285, ABB 283**, plus banks and chip firms.
- By industry it splits across **IT Services 1,510, Technology 1,448, BFSI 1,299, Semiconductors 807** — not a single-sector city.

A separate "India" bucket holds 2,324 roles that did not resolve to a city — nationwide or remote-tagged listings. Set those aside and Bengaluru's lead is starker still.

Bengaluru is hard to replace because it is not one industry stacking roles in one place: IT services, industrial engineering, hardware and semiconductors, global banks and consulting all run large operations here. If you are early-career, that breadth is worth a relocation. If you cannot move, target the second tier deliberately: Hyderabad for product and semiconductors, Pune for engineering and BFSI captives, Mumbai for finance — and remember the concentration cuts both ways, so a strong application into a smaller city can face a shallower pool. Set your target city honestly, then check how many roles there match your CV before you spend weeks applying.

## Methodology

Myro's jobs dataset, active roles read from company career pages, scrape window 27 April to 4 June 2026: 34,959 active roles across 247 employers. City counts use the resolved location field; a separate 2,324-role "India" bucket of unresolved postings is excluded from per-city shares. A point-in-time snapshot, not a complete count.
