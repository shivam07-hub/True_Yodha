If you build software, Bengaluru.

If you do front-office finance, Mumbai.

If you run a bank branch, stay where you are.

Our agents read 217 MNC career pages this month and pulled 14,851 live openings. We split every company by its industry, then cut each industry against the city it was hiring into. Here is the whole matrix.

| Industry | Openings | Bengaluru | Hyderabad | Mumbai | Delhi NCR | Pune | Chennai | Outside the big six |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consulting & Professional Services | 5,785 | 37.4% | 16.6% | 8.4% | 14.3% | 8.6% | 6.7% | 8.0% |
| BFSI | 4,973 | 15.1% | 6.1% | 16.5% | 9.1% | 6.9% | 3.9% | **42.4%** |
| Technology | 1,701 | **58.8%** | 20.0% | 2.9% | 4.2% | 4.8% | 2.5% | 6.7% |
| Diversified | 853 | 46.3% | 8.8% | 2.6% | 7.3% | 10.3% | 2.3% | 22.4% |
| Manufacturing & Industrial | 774 | 35.9% | 2.8% | 7.4% | 6.3% | 15.6% | 5.7% | 26.2% |
| Consumer & Retail | 374 | 13.9% | 15.2% | 19.0% | 8.6% | 1.6% | 1.9% | 39.8% |
| Healthcare & Life Sciences | 174 | 23.0% | 21.8% | 11.5% | 9.8% | 0.0% | 4.0% | 29.9% |

Each row adds to 100% across that industry's own openings. Read along your row.

## If you are an engineer, target Bengaluru and Hyderabad

Those two cities hold 78.8% of technology openings between them. Mumbai holds 2.9%, and that is not for lack of companies: 19 technology employers posted Mumbai roles against 49 in Bengaluru. They post 2.6 openings each in Mumbai and 20.4 each in Bengaluru. The offices are there. The hiring is not.

What those employers name, counted across their postings: Python (356), computer science fundamentals (255), artificial intelligence (229), C++ (187), electrical engineering (135), Java (120), design verification (70), parallel computing (62).

That list should change what you practise. C++, electrical engineering, design verification and parallel computing are not web-stack skills. A large share of Bengaluru's technology hiring is chip and systems work, which is why the Python-and-React path everyone recommends stops short of the biggest employers in the city.

## If you are in finance, which finance?

"Banking" in the table above is three different careers, and the city answer is different for each.

**Front-office finance is Mumbai.** Morgan Stanley posts 80% of its India roles there, and only 16% of them carry a technology title. CRISIL is 59% Mumbai, Deutsche Bank 41.5%, JP Morgan 35.4%. If you want to learn how money actually moves, that trading floor is in Mumbai and it is not being rebuilt anywhere else.

The Mumbai skill list reads accordingly: banking products (156), mutual funds (57), risk management (55), portfolio management (46), wealth management (44), compliance requirements (43), investment banking (41).

**Bank technology is Bengaluru and Pune.** Barclays' largest India metro is Pune, and 72% of its openings carry a technical title. BNY Mellon is Pune at 67% technical. JP Morgan's largest is Bengaluru at 56%. Wells Fargo and BNY Mellon posted zero Mumbai roles in this batch. The skills there are Python (120), regulatory compliance (109), SQL (92), Java (78), software engineering (66), Tableau (50). A bank in Bengaluru is a software company with a banking licence, and it hires like one.

**Branch banking is everywhere.** 42.4% of BFSI openings sit outside the six big metros: 2,107 roles, more than the entire national technology total of 1,701. Those postings ask for banking products (846), customer engagement (578), loans (378), mutual funds (360), cross-selling (349) and general insurance (241). None of that needs a Mumbai postcode, and moving for it buys you rent.

## The rest of the map

Consulting is the largest industry at 39.0% of all openings and behaves like technology: 37.4% Bengaluru, then Hyderabad at 16.6%. Manufacturing is the only row where Pune genuinely competes, at 15.6%. Consumer and retail is Mumbai's strongest row at 19.0%. Healthcare splits evenly between Bengaluru and Hyderabad on a base of 174 openings, so hold that row loosely.

One more reading. Technology has 60 employers here, more than any other industry, and 1,701 jobs. Consulting has 24 employers and 5,785. A sector having lots of companies hiring tells you nothing about how many jobs are in it.

## Methodology

14,851 job openings read from 217 companies' own career pages on 8 August 2026. Industry is a per-company attribute: every employer carries exactly one value, so no model is reading postings and guessing the sector.

Skill counts come from the skills named in the postings themselves. We drop generic filler and equal-opportunity boilerplate before counting, because otherwise "communication" and "diversity and inclusion" top every list in the country.

Metros are rolled up. Delhi NCR combines the Gurugram, Noida and Delhi labels, Thane counts with Mumbai. Two limits: this is 217 large employers with their own career sites, so startups and agency-only hiring are missing, and this is one date, so the shares hold better than the raw counts.
