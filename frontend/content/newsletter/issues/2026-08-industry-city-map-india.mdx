Almost nobody switches industry. People switch companies, cities and titles, and stay in the sector they already know. So the useful question is not what is hiring in India. It's where your industry hires, and whether that is anywhere near you.

We read 14,851 openings off 217 companies' own career pages. Here is every large industry, and the share of its openings each metro holds.

| Industry | Openings | Bengaluru | Hyderabad | Mumbai | Delhi NCR | Pune | Chennai | Outside the big six |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consulting & Professional Services | 5,785 | 37.4% | 16.6% | 8.4% | 14.3% | 8.6% | 6.7% | 8.0% |
| BFSI | 4,973 | 15.1% | 6.1% | 16.5% | 9.1% | 6.9% | 3.9% | **42.4%** |
| Technology | 1,701 | **58.8%** | 20.0% | 2.9% | 4.2% | 4.8% | 2.5% | 6.7% |
| Diversified | 853 | 46.3% | 8.8% | 2.6% | 7.3% | 10.3% | 2.3% | 22.4% |
| Manufacturing & Industrial | 774 | 35.9% | 2.8% | 7.4% | 6.3% | 15.6% | 5.7% | 26.2% |
| Consumer & Retail | 374 | 13.9% | 15.2% | 19.0% | 8.6% | 1.6% | 1.9% | 39.8% |
| Healthcare & Life Sciences | 174 | 23.0% | 21.8% | 11.5% | 9.8% | 0.0% | 4.0% | 29.9% |

Read your row, not the column totals. The row is your career.

## If you are in technology, your city is already decided

Technology puts 58.8% of its openings in Bengaluru and 20.0% in Hyderabad. Four in five technology jobs in this data sit in two cities. Mumbai holds 2.9% of them, which is 49 openings from the 60 technology employers on the list. If you are a developer in Mumbai waiting for the market to come to you, it is not coming. Bengaluru is not a preference for this industry, it is the industry.

## If you are in banking, stay where you are

Banking runs the opposite way. Mumbai is its biggest metro and holds only 16.5%, with Bengaluru a point and a half behind at 15.1%. The number that matters is the last column: 42.4% of BFSI openings sit outside the six big metros altogether. That is 2,107 jobs, more than the entire national technology total of 1,701.

This is the finding people get wrong most often. Everyone assumes finance means Mumbai. Branches are in every district, and the hiring follows the branches. If you are in banking in Indore or Coimbatore or Nagpur, moving to Mumbai buys you a small share of a market that is already coming to you.

## The rest of the map

Consulting is the biggest industry on the list at 39.0% of all openings, and it is a Bengaluru industry too: 37.4% there, then Hyderabad at 16.6% and Delhi NCR at 14.3%. Manufacturing is the one industry where Pune genuinely competes, taking 15.6% of it. Consumer and retail is Mumbai's strongest row at 19.0%, and healthcare splits almost evenly between Bengaluru and Hyderabad.

One number reframes the whole table. Technology has 60 employers here, more than any other industry, and 1,701 jobs. Consulting has 24 employers and 5,785. More companies hiring in your sector does not mean more jobs in it.

## What this means for you

**Early-career (0–3 years).** If you are in technology and not in Bengaluru or Hyderabad, relocation is the single biggest thing you can change, and it is worth doing before you have a mortgage. If you are in banking, the opposite holds: the roles are near you, and moving costs you rent for no gain in reach.

**Mid-career (3–10 years).** Check whether your industry's concentration matches your postcode before you blame your CV. A manufacturing manager in Pune and a technology lead in Bengaluru are both sitting in the right place. A technology lead in Mumbai is competing for 2.9% of their own industry.

**Senior (10+ years).** At your level the row matters less than who in it knows you. Use the table to pick which two cities your network should be in, then work it. In a 42.4%-outside industry like banking that network is national; in technology it is one city.

## The one move to make this week

Find your row. Compare the share your city holds to the share Bengaluru holds. If the gap is bigger than about ten points and you are early enough to move, the city is doing more damage to your search than anything on your CV.

## Methodology

14,851 job openings read from 217 companies' own career pages on 8 August 2026. Industry is a per-company attribute, one value per employer with no exceptions in this batch, so it does not depend on any model reading a posting.

Metros are rolled up: Delhi NCR combines Gurugram, Noida and Delhi labels, and Thane is counted with Mumbai. Without that rollup NCR appears three times in the table and reads smaller than it is.

Two caveats. This is 217 large employers with their own career sites, so startups, agencies and anything hiring only through job boards are absent, and the panel leans to consulting and BFSI. And a single date is a snapshot. Concentration this sharp does not move week to week, but the raw counts will.
