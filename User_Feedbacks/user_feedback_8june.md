HiMyro Platform Feedback
UX Evaluation & Core Functional Critique  |  Desktop & Mobile
🛠️ Critical Bugs & Usability Issues (Broken)
The Sign-Up form fields are completely hidden or not visible to users trying to register.
A technical issue prevents or restricts users from seamlessly selecting their intended target skills.
Users are artificially restricted to selecting a single location, with no ability to specify multiple geographical preferences.
The website currently lacks an established, navigable home page framework.
There are zero active social media links or channels embedded within the product ecosystem.
💡 Core Value Proposition (Useful)
The core concept is strong:
One place for multiple CV versions
Easier tailoring for different jobs
Centralized “career hub” positioning
The branding feels cleaner and more premium than typical resume builders.
“Career Intelligence” sounds broader than just resume storage, which is good for future expansion (job matching, AI optimization, portfolio, networking, etc.).
❓ Clarity & UX Friction (Confusing)
The biggest issue right now is clarity. When I land on the site, I still don’t immediately understand:
What exactly the product does
Why it’s better than ResumeBuilder and other legacy platforms
What the “aha moment” is
Who it’s primarily for:
students?
marketers?
tech professionals?
recruiters?
creators?
Right now it feels more like a concept than a workflow product.
