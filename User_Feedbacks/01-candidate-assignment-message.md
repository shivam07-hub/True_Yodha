# Myro Internship Assignment - Candidate Message

Replace `[DEADLINE]` and `[SUBMISSION LINK]` before sending.

**Subject: Myro internship assignment - test the product as a job seeker**

Hi,

Thank you for your interest in working with Myro.

Myro is a career intelligence platform that helps job seekers understand their
CV, identify skill gaps, find relevant roles, and decide what to improve next.

For this assignment, we want you to experience Myro as a real job seeker and
give us a clear, honest product assessment.

## Your assignment

Spend **30 minutes** using [Myro](https://www.himyro.com/) on the device you
normally use for job applications.

1. Open Myro and explore it without asking anyone from our team for guidance.
2. Upload your CV and continue until Myro gives you your first useful result.
3. Explore the CV analysis and at least one other relevant area, such as
   Skills, Jobs, Intel, Tracker, or CV tailoring.
4. Stop after 30 minutes. If something blocks you, do not fight it for hours;
   the blocker itself is useful feedback.
5. Submit your assessment using this link: **[SUBMISSION LINK]**

## What to submit

Keep your response concise. A document, presentation, or elaborate redesign is
not required.

### 1. Your understanding

In one or two sentences:

- What do you think Myro does?
- Who would benefit from it most?

### 2. Your most useful moment

Describe the one result or interaction that gave you the most value. Explain
why it mattered to you as a job seeker.

### 3. Your biggest problem

Describe the most confusing, inaccurate, slow, or frustrating moment.

Include:

- the page or feature;
- what you tried to do;
- what you expected;
- what actually happened; and
- your device and browser.

If you found a bug, add the shortest steps that reproduce it.

### 4. Your one priority improvement

If you could ask the Myro team to make only one improvement, what would it be?

Explain:

- which user problem it solves;
- your proposed change; and
- why this should be prioritized over other improvements.

A rough sketch or annotated screenshot is welcome when it makes the idea
clearer, but visual polish is not being evaluated.

### 5. What should remain unchanged

Name one part of Myro that the team should preserve and explain why.

### 6. Final rating

Rate each item from 1 to 5:

- I understood what to do next.
- I trusted the results shown to me.
- The results felt relevant to my career.
- I would use Myro again.
- I would recommend Myro to another job seeker.

Finally, complete this sentence:

> I would return to Myro when ______________________________.

## How we will evaluate the assignment

- **Specific observation - 30%:** You refer to real moments from your session,
  not generic advice.
- **Understanding of the user - 25%:** You explain why the issue or value
  matters to a job seeker.
- **Prioritization - 25%:** You choose one meaningful improvement and make a
  practical case for it.
- **Clarity - 20%:** Your response is concise, structured, and easy to
  understand.

We are **not** evaluating your CV, qualifications, Myro Score, design skills,
or whether your feedback is positive. Critical feedback will not reduce your
chances. Honest, specific disagreement is more useful than praise written to
impress us.

You may use spelling or formatting tools, but the observations and
recommendation must come from your own product session. Be prepared to explain
your response in a conversation.

## CV and privacy

- Upload only your own CV. You may remove your phone number, address, or other
  details that are not needed for the test.
- Do **not** attach your CV to the assignment response.
- Remove or blur personal information in screenshots.
- Myro does not share your CV with employers or recruiters and does not use
  uploaded content to train AI models. CV text may be processed by Myro's
  infrastructure and listed AI providers to generate results.
- You can review the [Privacy Policy](https://www.himyro.com/privacy) and
  [Terms of Service](https://www.himyro.com/terms) before participating.
- You may request access, export, correction, or deletion of your data by
  emailing `hello@himyro.com`.
