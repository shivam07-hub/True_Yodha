## LinkedIn

If you're applying to Citibank, the listing in front of you is almost certainly real. At AB InBev, 69.6% of closed roles are still advertised.

We checked ten global employers hiring in India against their own hiring systems. A role counts closed once the system stops serving it, and then we check whether the careers-page ad comes down with it.

Under 15% still advertised: Citibank 3.4%, Wells Fargo 5.1%, Amazon 10.6%, Barclays 13.2%.
Over 29%: Cisco 29.4%, Okta 39.5%, Palo Alto Networks 50.0%, AB InBev 69.6%.

Okta's dead ads stay up 42.4 days on average. Citibank's come down in 2.6.

Give the Ghost Index thirty seconds before you tailor a CV to a listing.
