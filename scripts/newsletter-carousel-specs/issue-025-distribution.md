If you want risk work in India, apply to the Big Four. EY posts 575 openings, Deloitte 242 and KPMG 126. More than half of EY's, 305, sit in one practice, Business Consulting Risk. They ask for regulation, audit and a CA: 25.9% of Big Four postings name the qualification, and 34.8% of EY's.

If you want a bank, Axis posts 231 risk openings, 37.2% of everything BFSI firms posted in the lane. 73.2% of them are in Mumbai. BFSI postings ask for Excel (30.0%), AML and KYC (19.6%) and credit risk (18.7%) more than the Big Four do.

If you came for risk analytics, 45 titles say analytics, 2.4% of the lane. Inside them, Python is in 51.1% and SAS in 33.3%. Across the whole lane, SAS and Basel appear in 2.8% of postings each.

If you are starting out, 9.6% of the lane is entry level, at 0.7 years. Axis posts 39 of those openings and EY 35.
