## LinkedIn

Want a bank job? 1,188 branch openings carry one title: Personal Banker. Kotak Mahindra Bank posts them, in 207 cities. Citibank and DBS post 10 more.

Our agents read MNC and bank career pages and pulled close to 59,000 openings in India. 5,649 of them were branch banking roles at Kotak Mahindra Bank and Axis Bank.

Five titles make up 67.2% of that branch hiring:

1. Personal Banker, Kotak: 1,188 openings in 207 cities. The postings ask for cross-selling, banking products and customer service. Only 4 of them state years of experience.
2. Elite Banker, Kotak: 1,180 openings in 157 cities. These ask for trade finance and commercial lending on top of sales and customer service. The 15 that state experience average 2.9 years.
3. Business Development Associate, Axis: 688 openings in 10 cities, every one entry level.
4. Branch Relationship Officer, Axis: 371 openings. None states years of experience.
5. Assistant Branch Manager, Kotak: 368 openings, at about four years' experience where stated.

If you are starting out, apply for Business Development Associate. It holds 688 of the 711 entry-level branch openings, and only Axis posts it. Every posting asks for a graduate degree and one year of experience, and names sales, lead generation, business development and communication.

Those openings sit in 10 cities. Mumbai has 169 and NCR (Delhi, Gurugram and Noida) more than 170, about half between them. Bengaluru has 87, Kolkata 69, and Ahmedabad and Chennai 67 each. In every one of these cities the entry-level role is the same: Business Development Associate.

After that first job, your city decides which bank is hiring you. Axis posts 76.4% of Kolkata's branch openings and 68.9% of Chennai's. Kotak posts 57.3% of Pune's. Outside Axis's 10 cities, every branch opening we found is Kotak's: 1,350 of them.
