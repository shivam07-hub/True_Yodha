## LinkedIn

The Big Four post 65.4% of India's technology-risk roles, and 58.2% of them ask for an engineering degree.

We read every live risk and compliance role on 260+ MNC career pages in India on 16 November 2026. 292 of 1,113 are technology-risk roles: IT audit, GRC, third-party risk, cyber, data privacy, identity and access.

If you are a software engineer looking for a way into risk, this is the seat. Deloitte posts 131 of these roles, KPMG 31 and EY 29. At the Big Four, 72.3% of them ask for an engineering degree.

Cloud appears in 49.3% of these roles and identity and access in 39.4%, against 15.5% and 11.3% across the rest of the lane. Python appears in 8.6%.

The new layer is one framework and one certificate. ISO 27001 and CISSP are each named in 102 roles, CISA in 95 and NIST in 87. Credit risk, the usual advice for this move, appears in 1 of the 292.

Of the 200 roles that state experience, 130 (65.0%) ask for five years or less. 13 of the 292 are entry level.
