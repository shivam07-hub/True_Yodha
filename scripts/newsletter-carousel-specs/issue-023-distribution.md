## LinkedIn

Citibank pulls a closed listing in 2.6 days on average. AB InBev still advertises 69.6% of roles its hiring system has already closed.

We read ten India career pages against the hiring system behind them, through 24 September 2026. Four employers sit under 15% still advertised. Four sit over 29%.

On the cleaner four, the live postings name SQL (57), regulatory compliance (55), Java (49), Python (40) and supply chain (12). On the slower four: Python (90), the Okta platform (37), artificial intelligence (30), computer science fundamentals (27), software engineering (23).

The World Economic Forum's Future of Jobs survey, January 2025, names Big Data Specialists, AI and Machine Learning Specialists, and Security Management Specialists as the fastest-growing roles on its India page. That is their expectation for 2030. The application is the posting the career page still serves, a CV tailored to the skills that posting names, and a submit on that page.

### One caption, for the laptop

You may be tailoring a CV for a job that's already closed. AB InBev still advertised 69.6% of the India roles its hiring system had closed, through 24 September 2026.

Whether it's still open depends on the employer: Palo Alto Networks still advertised 50.0% of its closed India roles, Barclays 13.2%.

Closing the role and pulling the ad are two teams' jobs at most companies, and one of them is slower.

So you can spend an evening tailoring a CV for a role that's already filled. At AB InBev, a closed role's ad stayed up 11.5 days on average.

Check the employer's own hiring system before you tailor, not just the careers page.

## Demo storyboard

No video file. Six beats, screen recording, the person's own hands on the last two.

1. The Ghost Index table. Finger on Citibank 3.4% and AB InBev 69.6%.
2. A role card whose check says the listing is open. The judge score is on the card.
3. A CV tailored to that role. The skills on the CV are the skills the posting named.
4. Approve this CV for filing. The line on screen: approved, and the form is still unsent.
5. The company careers page. The extension fills email, phone, and the answers the CV contains. It leaves a blank where the CV has no answer. It does not press submit.
6. The person presses submit. The confirmation page is on that same host. Back in Myro, the role reads applied.

A login screen, a captcha, or a blank question ends the recording on that page, with the person still holding it.
