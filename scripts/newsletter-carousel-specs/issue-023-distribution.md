On the cleaner four, the live postings name SQL (57), regulatory compliance (55), Java (49), Python (40) and supply chain (12). On the slower four: Python (90), the Okta platform (37), artificial intelligence (30), computer science fundamentals (27), software engineering (23).

## Demo storyboard

No video file. Six beats, screen recording, the person's own hands on the last two.

1. The Ghost Index table. Finger on Citibank 3.4% and AB InBev 69.6%.
2. A role card whose check says the listing is open. The judge score is on the card.
3. A CV tailored to that role. The skills on the CV are the skills the posting named.
4. Approve this CV for filing. The line on screen: approved, and the form is still unsent.
5. The company careers page. The extension fills email, phone, and the answers the CV contains. It leaves a blank where the CV has no answer. It does not press submit.
6. The person presses submit. The confirmation page is on that same host. Back in Myro, the role reads applied.

A login screen, a captcha, or a blank question ends the recording on that page, with the person still holding it.
